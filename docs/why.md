# Why obligations is one theory

## The scale

Every quantity a system answers for has two marks on one scale. The floor is what must hold; the ceiling is what may
hold. A cell of the world is the pair, and nothing smaller is worth naming: a floor alone says nothing about what is
forbidden, a ceiling alone says nothing about what is owed. Between the two marks lie the states still undecided, and
their count, in bits, is how much freedom the cell has left.

A second origin that touches the same cell does not overwrite the first. The two bounds meet: the floor rises to the
higher of the two, the ceiling falls to the lower. If the marks cross, the cell is in conflict, and the conflict is a
state of its own, not an error to be hidden. Everything the library computes is this meeting, read one way or another.

## One lattice, read through its views

The pairs live on a lattice, and the lattice is the only primitive. What looks like many theories is the same meet and
join asked different questions:

- as a **chain**, how finely a cell is resolved and how it moves along its scale;
- as **ideals**, what a cell requires and what it permits, as an upset and a downset;
- as a **field**, where a bound is held, at what resolution, since when and by which origins;
- as a **valuation**, what a bound is worth, and what planning buys over retreating;
- as a **spectrum**, which values can occur at all, and where an impossibility lives;
- as a **product**, how a region of many cells decomposes into parts that do not interact;
- as a **quotient**, what a coarser reading cannot tell apart, and the coarsest reading that still moves a verdict;
- as a **complement**, what can be taken back exactly, and where a clash is a plain contradiction;
- as its **dual**, what the permitted side says on its own;
- as a **Galois connection**, what is determined by what.

No view adds algebra. An operation belongs to the view whose laws its vectors fix, and a law belongs to the view in
which it is read.

Receipts compose in any order, and the fold of a union is the meet of the folds. That is the meet's own indifference to
order read on receipts, one step from it, and nothing rests on it: a remark here, not a law of the lock.

A field whose cells fold themselves was measured once, by one origin, to become honest without becoming agreed: its
promises drew together while its states kept their counts. No operation of this package reproduces that field yet, so
it is a reading here and not a law, and it enters the lock the day evolve run over selfFold gives the same.

## Why three bodies of work are one theory

The price of a declaration, the debit of a cell and the field of origins were written apart, each with its own
vocabulary. They are one theory because each is a reading of the same pair on the same lattice. The price is the pair
read through a valuation: what violating a bound costs, and what the premium of planning is. The debit is the pair
read through its ideals and its chain: four states per irreducible, and the bits between floor and ceiling. The field
is the pair laid over coordinates and origins: where it is held, when, and by whom, and what an encounter of two
origins makes of it.

Stated once, in `src/laws/laws.bound`, each law carries its statement, what it rests on, the view it is read in, its
case and, where it can be falsified, its falsifier. This page argues why the laws belong together; it counts nothing
that the render of the lock already counts.
