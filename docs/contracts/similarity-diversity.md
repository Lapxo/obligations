# Similarity diversity

Maximum order-2 similarity diversity over a finite declared population; exact rational exhaustive-support certificate. The normalized symmetric similarity domain is the domain of the cited common-maximum theorem; no covariance conversion is implicit.

Inputs: distinct source ids; symmetric rational matrix; entries in [0,1]; diagonal exactly 1; nonempty population; declared rational-update budget.

Copies may receive zero in the chosen optimizer representative, but every original source id remains in parameters and weights. A zero weight does not withdraw a declaration.

A missing model or required evidence produces `unmeasured` with a cause. Invalid declared data refuses; it is never clipped, normalized or converted silently. The host separately bounds parsing, process lifetime and input bytes. The mathematical work budget does not bound arbitrary-precision bit complexity or wall time.

Does not establish: covariance-to-similarity conversion; causal independence; number of shared latent causes; probability of decision error; error-optimal voting; unique optimizer; admission authority.

The output preserves the supplied population and model. a structured-data consumer represents it; a constraint evaluator judges authority, budgets and admissibility. The finite vectors are acceptance tests, not a rerun of the received scientific campaign.
