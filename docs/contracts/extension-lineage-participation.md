# Extension, named lineage and effective participation

Extension is the supplied set of coordinate/interval pairs in A5, compared at fixed coordinate interpretation, resolution and context. Equal extensions identify one extensional class under that convention. They do not identify historical actors, establish a shared cause or prove probabilistic independence. A named lineage is a declared model grouping with a supplied generative/ancestry mechanism, membership and weights. Its name is not observational evidence.

Joint independence is factorization of the declared joint probability law for all joint events. A conditional statement names its conditioning variable and requires factorization in every positive-mass stratum. Different names, disjoint extensions and pairwise zero covariance do not suffice. The fair-bit XOR triple has independent pairs and fails joint factorization.

For finite nonnegative masses with positive total, set w_i=a_i/sum(a) and K_eff=1/sum(w_i^2). It is dimensionless weight participation, between1 and the positive-support size; equal positive weights give that size. It is not a count of causally independent extensions. For a symmetric PSD covariance C with positive trace, normalized nonnegative eigenvalues instead give spectral participation trace(C)^2/tr(C^2). These are different inputs and need a model before they can be equated. Existing participationRatio checks symmetry and nonnegative diagonal but does not certify PSD. A true covariance constructed from the declared error rows supplies PSD; passing arbitrary matrices directly does not. Zero variation is unmeasured. In O4, K_eff=K and identification from population moments apply only in the declared uniform known-p family; nonuniform moment collisions remain.

A7 concerns fixed coordinates, resolution, context and ceilings under added live narrowing constraints. Withdrawal and witnessed local widening may reopen freedom on the same coordinate. A sum of coordinate log-cardinal freedoms is not automatically joint Shannon entropy. T90 equates log-cardinality with entropy for nonempty finite uniformly distributed admissible states at fixed resolution; it does not establish the identity for arbitrary priors or a physical phase interpretation.

A9 is a representation convention, not a convergence theorem. T99 concerns finite records and worlds, an acyclic reachable dependency graph and total supplied lattice operations: evaluation takes finitely many operations. Reachable ancestral cycles reject, while unreachable cycles do not affect a query. An arbitrary monotone iteration still needs its carrier, operator, seed and progress/height hypotheses; a monotone reflection on incomparable singleton intervals has a two-cycle. Different hypotheses and missing proofs are not a fork.

The historical C1 independence-is-distance wording supplies no probabilistic factorization. Definitions settle meaning only. Conditional lineage identification, causal subgroup recovery and oracle-free reference remain open. This companion text changes no signed base, algebra, M/3-M/4 or wire.
