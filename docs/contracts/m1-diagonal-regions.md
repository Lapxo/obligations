In the declared binary M1 ancestor-first fixed-quota model, a latent configuration with every q_i=1/2 has fair-majority error exactly1/2 at every finiteN. Thus the complete implementation and primitive-generator mixture converge to the latent threshold functional whenever every latent configuration is either off diagonal or entirely unbiased. A two-level example p=3/4,c1=1/5,c2=1/6,one ancestor and one leaf has a positive-mass unbiased diagonal state and a Lean-proved full-generator error limit1/32. For heterogeneous diagonal configurations, positive fresh massd=(1-c1)(1-c2) gives variance>=N*d^2*p*(1-p), bounded quota drift, vanishing standardized threshold and the checked bounded-array Lindeberg condition for epsilon>0. These are prerequisites, not a proved general CLT. Transfer from actual primitive fixed-cut CDF limits to the moving majority cut is proved only with that distributional convergence supplied explicitly. The general heterogeneous-diagonal probability limit remains open, as do the three original research cards.
