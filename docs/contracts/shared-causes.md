`identifyM3` consumes a known incidence family and oracle population moments. Its positive, full-column-rank result is conditional on that family and its hypotheses. It retains source/factor ids and unresolved private/singleton survival. The received T5 latent-copy/noise model is a different family: its reported runs are preserved as received, without a new experiment, reconstructed archive, or universal recovery theorem.

The fixed acceptance vectors show: exact recovery in an existing known-incidence case; rank deficiency despite n=4 and two duplicate factor columns; equal conditional pairs with different full information; unresolved private versus singleton components; and similarity diversity 8/5 for one declared shared signal with independent noise. The latter uses an explicitly declared centered fair-sign conversion, never a covariance silently treated as a normalized similarity.
