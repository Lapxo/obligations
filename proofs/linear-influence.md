# Finite noiseless linear influence: derivations and limits

Use a fixed finite population with n>0, mean m(x)=sum(x)/n and Cov_n(a,x)=m(ax)-m(a)m(x). The executable witnesses use exact rational coordinates. Nonnegative row-stochastic W and alpha in [0,1]^n define the declared application domain; the algebraic identities often hold on a wider scalar domain.

For W=J and zero noise, sum((1-a_i)x_i+a_i*m(x))/n-m(x) equals -Cov_n(a,x). Constant alpha makes this covariance zero. These identities and the column-sum transport formula are kernel-checked in LinearInfluence.lean. For homogeneous alpha and any row-stochastic W, sum_i sum_j W_ij*x_j equals sum_j c_j*x_j with c=W^T*1 and mean(c)=1. The drift is therefore a*Cov_n(c,x). Degree cannot replace c for a general neighbor-average operator. A declared normalized nonnegative left-stationary vector w of W preserves its weighted mean under homogeneous a; the unnormalized weighted-sum identity is also kernel-checked. It need not remain stationary after introducing nonconstant alpha. Vector alpha instead requires the full expression sum_i alpha_i*((Wx)_i-x_i)/n.

For complete mixing and constant a, set P=I-J and q=(1-a)^k, k=t-1>=0. J^2=J, P^2=P and JP=PJ=0 imply A^k=J+qP and x_t=m(x_1)*1+q*(x_1-m(x_1)*1). A common affine transformation carries every median interval to its affine image for nonnegative q. Convergence to the mean requires 0<a<=1; a=0 need not converge. This is a reviewed affine derivation, not a kernel-checked real convergence theorem.

If Sigma_1=s*I with s>0, no noise and that same deterministic A, covariance transport gives Sigma_t=s*(J+q^2*P), trace=s*(1+(n-1)*q^2), and 1^T*Sigma_t*1=s*n. Consequently K_corr=n*trace/(1^T*Sigma*1)=1+(n-1)*q^2. The scalar normalization and scaling invariance are kernel-checked; the covariance-transport and projector derivation above is not claimed as a full Lean probability formalization. Covariance must be declared, not inferred from keys or grouping. Negative correlations can give K_corr>n: Gram rows (1,0),(-1,1) give Sigma=[[1,-1],[-1,2]], trace=3, sum=1 and K_corr=6. Gram rows (1),(-1) give zero denominator and no invented size.

T2, stochastic recurrences, arbitrary-W threshold claims and asynchronous members are excluded. K4, G3, A4 and H1-H3 still need their exact source propositions; a label or input tuple is not an audited refutation.
