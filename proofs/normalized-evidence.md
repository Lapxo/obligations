# Conditional evidence and its declared reference

The function computes KL(P||Q)=sum P(x) log2(P(x)/Q(x)) for the two supplied conditional laws and divides by the same oriented divergence of one named coordinate marginal. Both masses are validated as exact rational probabilities with total exactly one. Zero P contributes zero; positive P with Q=0 has incompatible support and is unmeasured rather than a fabricated finite ratio. A zero or numerically unresolved reference divergence cannot supply a number. Logarithms and the ratio are explicitly binary64 approximations, without a directed-rounding certificate.

An injective duplication x -> (x,x) preserves every positive and negative conditional mass. Group KL and the chosen source marginal KL are unchanged; copy count does not increase N. For conditionally independent blocks, log of the likelihood product is the sum of log likelihoods, and expectation gives additive unnormalized group KL. Normalized ratios add only when their denominators are the same compatible reference. Distinct key or lineage metadata does not establish this factorization.

Fixed acceptance tables use a source with P=(3/4,1/4), Q=(1/4,3/4); its divergence is (1/2)log2(3). Two declared independent copies of the channel give twice that divergence and N=2. Two exact coordinate duplicates give N=1. These finite tables validate the function, not an empirical independence inference.

There is no universal N<=n under heterogeneous reference laws. With independent conditional sources A and B, A has P_A=(2/3,1/3), Q_A=(1/3,2/3), so KL_A=1/3 bit. B has P_B=(9/10,1/10), Q_B=(1/10,9/10), so KL_B=(4/5)log2(9). Taking A as reference gives N=1+3*(4/5)log2(9)>2. The exact product probabilities used by the test are (3/5,1/15,3/10,1/30) and (1/30,3/10,1/15,3/5). No clipping to source count is permitted.

N is not thereby an admissible valuation of the lattice paper. Its approximation by similarity diversity, finite upper bounds in specific copy models, and the received T9/T10 results retain their stated domains and evidence status. No scientific experiment is run or promoted here.
