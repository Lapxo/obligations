Within the declared domain of distinct nonempty claim ids, every historical claim retains its origin, id, epoch and span. Equal spans do not merge authors. Withdrawing a named id removes exactly that live line while history remains available.

Origin is attribution, not a key certificate. Distinct ids, keys or lineage labels do not identify independent causes; authority stays with the consumer instrument. No lineage recovery is inferred.

The deterministic reader consumes the declared finite fixture and its sealed expected answer. It does not authenticate or admit a claim. The contract, positive and negative in each of two unrelated contexts, and captured process output are bound by digest. No scientific experiment is run.

## Justification

For claim ids a and b with equal values, the withdrawal set contains a alone. Existing `live` tests membership by id, so b remains; `observe` appends the withdrawal to history. Swapping equal values or grouping a measurement does not provide authority to withdraw b. The published key-lineage countermodels already show why attribution alone does not prove factorization.
