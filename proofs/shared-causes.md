## Conditional information bound

For one declared finite joint prior and a Markov channel Y -> S=(S_1,...,S_L) -> X, Shannon data processing gives I(Y;X)<=I(Y;S). If the S_l are jointly independent conditioned on Y, H(S|Y)=sum H(S_l|Y), while H(S)<=sum H(S_l). Subtracting gives I(Y;S)<=sum I(Y;S_l).
